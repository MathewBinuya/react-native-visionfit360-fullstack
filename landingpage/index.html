What it does

Everything you need to train smarter

VisionFIT360 brings guidance, tracking, and motivation together in one app — so you can work out correctly, even without a trainer.

AR rep counter

Point your camera and start moving. RepVision tracks your joints and counts every rep automatically across 12 exercises.

Real-time form check

A skeletal overlay turns green for good form and red when something's off — clear guidance you can read from across the room.

AI workout coach

Get personalized workout suggestions based on your goals and activity level, powered by AI.

Workout tracker

Log routines with sets, reps, weight, and rest. Build sessions and keep your training organized.

Progress & history

Review past sessions and track your volume over time so you can see yourself improving week to week.

Daily reminders

Gentle nudges and motivation keep you consistent, so workout days don't slip by unnoticed.

How it works

Three steps to your first guided rep

Pick an exercise

Open RepVision and choose from 12 guided exercises, each with quick form tips before you start.

Stand in frame

Prop up your phone, step back so your body's in view, and the camera starts tracking your movement instantly.

Move and improve

Watch your reps count up and your form light up green or red — real-time feedback with every movement.
